START HERE
Option A – interactive: open PeriodCloseAgent_HOL_Wizard.html in Edge or Chrome. It walks you
   through every step with checklists, copy buttons for agent prompts, enlargeable screenshots and
   built-in file downloads. Progress is saved in your browser.
Option B – document:
1. Open HOL_PeriodCloseAgent_External.docx. If the page numbers in the Contents show 1, right-click the
   Contents and select Update Field > Update entire table (or press F9 in Word).
2. Follow Part 1 (install and configure), Part 2 (run close scenarios) and Part 3 (add business skills).
3. Appendix B covers troubleshooting and how to reset the lab for the next learner.

BEFORE YOU RUN THE LAB IN YOUR OWN TENANT
- Create your own SharePoint site and folders (Step 7) and enter its URL in the four skills (Step 12).
  The solution ships with the placeholder {{SHAREPOINT_SITE_URL}} in those four skills; replace it with
  your site URL.
- Give each Responsible worker on agent-enabled tasks an email address, or review emails are not sent.
- Scenario 2 (Accounts payable currency revaluation) posts directly. Use a test environment.
- Your administrator enables the Period Close Agent capability, the ERP MCP server and the virtual tables.
- Production use is gated and controlled by Microsoft. Microsoft enables the Period Close Agent capability
  for production environments on request; register through https://aka.ms/PeriodCloseAgentSignUp.
  Lab, evaluation and sandbox environments do not need this. This gating will change as the capability matures.
