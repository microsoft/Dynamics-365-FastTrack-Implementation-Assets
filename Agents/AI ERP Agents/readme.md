# AI ERP Agent Templates

Explore agent templates for Dynamics 365 ERP scenarios, from finance and order
processing to retail operations and administration. These examples provide
starting points for learning, evaluation, and adaptation to your organization's
business processes.

Each template has its own scope, prerequisites, and setup guidance. Open an
agent's README below to explore its capabilities and available artifacts.

## Before you get started

1. **Choose a scenario.** Read the template's overview to understand its intended
   use and limitations.
2. **Check the prerequisites.** Review the required applications, licensing,
   permissions, connections, and environment configuration in that template's
   documentation.
3. **Evaluate before adopting.** Use a suitable nonproduction environment,
   review security and human-approval requirements, and validate behavior before
   adapting the template for your organization.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Templates differ in maturity and available artifacts. Inclusion in this catalog
> does not mean a template is ready for production. Some agents can create or
> change business data or provision access; review their instructions and controls
> before enabling them.
